Common rejection reasons in Oakland Park

29 documented. Our analysis checks your package against these.

1Insurance certificate holder MUST read EXACTLY: 'City of Oakland Park, 1100 Park Lane East, First Floor, Oakland Park, FL 33334' — confirmed from CURRENT Contractor Registration Requirements PDF AND Pool/Spa Packet (both updated). Older permit packets (dock, seawall, driveway) still show outdated addresses. The current registration PDF is authoritative.
2ALL applications MUST use the Broward County Uniform Building Permit Application (Rev 11/14/25) — no other form accepted. Old revisions rejected. Download current version from city website or Broward County.
3ALL applications and documents must be completely filled out, SIGNED, and NOTARIZED — unnotarized applications rejected at intake.
4HVHZ city — ALL exterior product approvals MUST be Miami-Dade County NOAs (Notice of Acceptance). Florida Product Approvals alone NOT sufficient. Expired NOAs = automatic rejection.
5NOC threshold is $5,000 for all trades EXCEPT HVAC which is $15,000. NOC must be recorded through Broward County BEFORE submitting to Oakland Park. One recorded copy required before first inspection.
6Online submittals — ALL documents must be PDF format ONLY (no img, jpeg, tiff). Plans as ONE combined PDF. Application as SEPARATE PDF. Do NOT lock, restrict, or add security to PDFs — prevents staff from opening/stamping.
7Digital seals required for online submittals — plans must be DIGITALLY signed by design professional. Wet ink on scanned copy NOT accepted for digital submittals.
8Roofing permits require BOTH the Broward County Uniform Application AND the Roofing HVHZ-Uniform Permit Application (Rev 2023). Submitting only one = incomplete.
9HVAC permits require the BORA AC Replacements Form (Policy 19-02) — EACH SYSTEM requires its own form. SB 612 changed line-side electrical scope effective 7/1/2024.
10Contractor must be REGISTERED with City of Oakland Park BEFORE pulling any permit — registration requires: (1) Signed/notarized registration form, (2) State cert or Broward County COC, (3) County BTR, (4) COI naming City as cert holder at 1100 Park Lane East, First Floor, (5) Qualifier's state driver license.
11EPD/EPGMD approval required BEFORE building dept for: new buildings, demolitions, additions to non-res, interior alterations with change of use, commercial re-roofs, slabs > 250 sq ft, detached additions. Must apply for permit within 30 DAYS of EPD approval or it expires.
12Work without permit = DOUBLE permit fee + work may be partially or completely demolished.
13Inspection requests must be in portal by 3:00 PM for next business day. After 3 PM = scheduled for SECOND business day.
14Expired permit renewal is TIERED: <30 days = 25% of original fee ($120 min); 30-59 days = 50% ($120 min); 60-89 days = 75% ($120 min); 90+ days = 100% ($120 min). This is NOT a flat fee.
15Re-inspection fees: First re-inspection = $90. SUBSEQUENT re-inspection for SAME violation = $320. Per F.S. 553.80(2)(c).
16Emergency AC replacement MAY start before permit approval ONLY with Chief Inspector approval (up to first required inspection only). Must still apply for permit.
17Fence heights in Oakland Park R-1/R-2: max 5' in front yards, 6'-½" (six feet, half inch) all other locations. Up to 8' only if abutting non-residential/multifamily district. R-1A/R-1B/R-1C: max 6' (8' abutting commercial/industrial). Barbed wire, razor wire, electrified wire PROHIBITED.
18Fence bordering a waterway requires Drainage District Approval Letter AND triggers Engineering review stop — contractors frequently miss this.
19Seawall spot survey must be approved by Broward County Building Department BEFORE placing tie beam concrete.
20Dock and seawall permits require multi-agency approval: City + Broward County EPGMD + FDEP + USACE (Army Corps). Must comply with Ch. 14 of Oakland Park Code of Ordinances.
21Asbestos — Statement of Responsibility Regarding Asbestos Form required for ALL renovations and demolitions, not just pre-1981 buildings.
22Owner-builder must personally appear at building dept to sign application. Notarized Owner Builder Disclosure Statement required. Best used for small jobs (patios, fences, walkways) — complex jobs problematic.
23Projects over $200,000 require non-refundable deposit per dry run table per trade. Deposit credited to final permit cost but NOT refundable if project doesn't proceed.
24Credit card payments incur processing fee: $2.50 for transactions under $66.67; 3.75% for transactions over $66.67.
25Tree removal requires permit: $41 application fee + $20/tree under 18" DBH or $66/tree over 18" DBH. After-the-fact fees are DOUBLED per tree.
26Driveway Swale Acknowledgment is NOT just a form — it must be completed, notarized, AND RECORDED in Official Records of Broward County at owner's expense BEFORE permit issuance. Plus a Swale Inspection is a required inspection.
27Change of Contractor requires: Hold Harmless/Indemnity Letter (notarized) + NEW permit application + possible amended NOC. All work SUSPENDED until new contractor obtains new permit. This is NOT a simple paperwork swap.
28Electrical Service Form requires NOTARY SEAL in addition to approval by Electrical Division and licensed electrical contractor.
29Pool permits have up to EIGHT review stops (Electrical, Engineering, Flood, Mechanical, Plumbing, Structural, Zoning, Fire Marshall) — plan for extended review timeline on pool projects.
Frequently asked questions
Where do I submit a permit application in Oakland Park?

Online preferred (PDF only) + In-Person at 1100 Park Lane East, 1st Floor. Portal: https://oaklandparkfl-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/SelfService#/home.

What insurance certificate holder wording does Oakland Park require?

The certificate holder should read: City of Oakland Park, 1100 Park Lane East, First Floor, Oakland Park, FL 33334

Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Oakland Park?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Oakland Park building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

How many plan sets does Oakland Park require?

2 sets listed. This can vary by permit type, so confirm with the building department.

Is Oakland Park in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ)?

Yes. Oakland Park is in the HVHZ, which affects product approval and wind load requirements.
