Frequently asked questions
Where do I submit a permit application in Pompano Beach?

100% Electronic via ePlan - Applications must be in BLACK INK. Portal: https://c2g.pompanobeachfl.gov/Click2GovBP/.

What insurance certificate holder wording does Pompano Beach require?

The certificate holder should read: City of Pompano Beach, 100 W Atlantic Blvd, Pompano Beach, FL 33060

Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Pompano Beach?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Pompano Beach building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

How many plan sets does Pompano Beach require?

1 set listed. This can vary by permit type, so confirm with the building department.

Is Pompano Beach in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ)?

Yes. Pompano Beach is in the HVHZ, which affects product approval and wind load requirements.

What are common reasons permits get rejected in Pompano Beach?

Applications MUST be in BLACK INK - will be rejected otherwise. Fire Review Application required for multi-family/condo/commercial — NOT required for single-family residential. Broward County EPD must be approved BEFORE city submittal — submitting without EPD = rejection.
