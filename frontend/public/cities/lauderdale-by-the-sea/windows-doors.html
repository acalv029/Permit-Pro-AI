Window & Door Permit checklist for Lauderdale-by-the-Sea
  • Submit via CitizenServe online portal
  • Broward County Uniform Building Permit Application — signed and notarized
  • Permit Affidavit for HOA compliance — notarized
  • Construction Time Limits form
  • LBTS Window/Door Schedule sheet — floor plan with opening ID must be submitted
  • All retrofit windows/doors/shutters/garage doors must meet wind load requirements via one of three methods:
  • Option 1: Site-specific plan signed/sealed by PE or Architect with location of all openings and design pressures
  • Option 2: Site-specific plan with worst-case design pressure chart signed/sealed by PE or Architect
  • Option 3: Site-specific plan with design pressures based on FBC Section 1609
  • Building layout with all measurements showing locations and sizes of ALL windows/doors to be replaced
  • NOA highlighted showing the specific windows/doors to be used and tested wind pressures for each — must show compliance with FBC Chapter 16
  • Zone 4 vs Zone 5 determination required — Zone 5 is any opening within corner distance (10% of least horizontal dimension or 40% of mean roof height, min 3 ft)
  • TURTLE LIGHTING REQUIREMENT (LBTS-SPECIFIC): Can any part of dwelling unit be seen from the beach?
  • If YES: ALL windows and doors must have tinted glass with Visible Light Transmitting Value (VLT) <= 0.45
  • Applies to seaward AND shore-perpendicular sides of structure
  • Must submit documented proof that glass meets VLT <= 0.45 requirement
  • Both owner and contractor must sign acknowledgment of turtle lighting compliance
  • Insurance must list: 'Town of Lauderdale By-The-Sea, 4501 N. Ocean Drive, Lauderdale By-The-Sea, FL 33308'
  • NOC required if work value over $5,000
  • GotchaTURTLE LIGHTING — if visible from beach, ALL glass must be VLT <= 0.45. This is unique to LBTS and catches contractors by surprise
  • GotchaNOA must be highlighted showing specific product and tested pressures — must demonstrate compliance per zone
  • GotchaHVHZ — all products must have valid Miami-Dade NOA with wind pressures meeting Broward 170 mph requirements
  • GotchaMust identify each opening (1A, 1B, 1C format) on schedule with zone, product approval number, glass type, LTV%, U-factor, SHGC, and required vs actual pressures
  • NoteMiscellaneous permit fee = 2% of construction cost; minimum $85.00
Requirements change. Confirm the current checklist with the Lauderdale-by-the-Sea building department before you submit. See every permit type on the Lauderdale-by-the-Sea permit requirements page.
Frequently asked questions
Where do I submit a permit application in Lauderdale-by-the-Sea?

Online preferred via CitizenServe - paper accepted at Town Hall if unable to submit online. Portal: https://www2.citizenserve.com/Portal/PortalController?Action=showHomePage&installationID=117.

What insurance certificate holder wording does Lauderdale-by-the-Sea require?

The certificate holder should read: Town of Lauderdale By-The-Sea, 4501 N. Ocean Drive, Lauderdale By-The-Sea, FL 33308

Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Lauderdale-by-the-Sea?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Lauderdale-by-the-Sea building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

How many plan sets does Lauderdale-by-the-Sea require?

2 sets listed. This can vary by permit type, so confirm with the building department.

Is Lauderdale-by-the-Sea in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ)?

Yes. Lauderdale-by-the-Sea is in the HVHZ, which affects product approval and wind load requirements.

What gets a window & door application rejected in Lauderdale-by-the-Sea?

TURTLE LIGHTING — if visible from beach, ALL glass must be VLT <= 0.45. This is unique to LBTS and catches contractors by surprise. NOA must be highlighted showing specific product and tested pressures — must demonstrate compliance per zone. HVHZ — all products must have valid Miami-Dade NOA with wind pressures meeting Broward 170 mph requirements.
