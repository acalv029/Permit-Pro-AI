Building Permit checklist for Sunrise
  • Submit permit application through Customer Self Service Portal at sunrisefl.gov/openforbusiness OR in person at 10770 W. Oakland Park Blvd, Sunrise, FL 33351
  • GotchaALL permit applications must FIRST be submitted to Sunrise Zoning — Signed Zoning Checklist is REQUIRED or application is REJECTED
  • GotchaDemos, additions, alterations, new construction must be approved by Broward County ePermits FIRST (broward.org/epermits), then Sunrise Community Development for exterior changes, THEN Building Division
  • Re-roofing, interior renovations, fencing, interior plumbing/electrical, AC changeouts go DIRECTLY to Building Division — no Planning review needed
  • Hours: Mon–Thu 8 AM – 5 PM, Fri 8 AM – 4 PM

Frequently asked questions
Where do I submit a permit application in Sunrise?

e-Plan Review online + in-person; SIGNED ZONING CHECKLIST REQUIRED — applications rejected without it. Portal: https://sunrisefl.gov/openforbusiness.

Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Sunrise?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Sunrise building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

How many plan sets does Sunrise require?

2 sets listed. This can vary by permit type, so confirm with the building department.

Is Sunrise in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ)?

Yes. Sunrise is in the HVHZ, which affects product approval and wind load requirements.

What gets a building application rejected in Sunrise?
