Frequently asked questions
Where do I submit a permit application in Miami?

FULLY DIGITAL — ALL applications via iBuild; plans via ProjectDox/ePlan. NO PAPER APPLICATIONS.. Portal: https://www.miami.gov/Permits-Construction/iBuild-Portal-Link.

Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Miami?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Miami building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

How many plan sets does Miami require?

2 sets listed. This can vary by permit type, so confirm with the building department.

Is Miami in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ)?

Yes. Miami is in the HVHZ, which affects product approval and wind load requirements.

What are common reasons permits get rejected in Miami?

ALL permit applications are 100% DIGITAL via iBuild — NO paper applications accepted. Plans must be uploaded through ePlan/ProjectDox — NOT submitted through iBuild directly. Plans must be digitally signed and sealed — printed copies are NOT considered signed and sealed.
