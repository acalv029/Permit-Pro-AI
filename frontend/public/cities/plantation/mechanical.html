Mechanical / HVAC Permit checklist for Plantation
  • Broward County Uniform Permit Application with Mechanical box checked — signed and notarized by Qualifier
  • Submit digitally via Accela Citizen Access (ACA) — digital ONLY
  • File naming: ME-HVAC, ME-Alteration, ME-Hood, ME-Fire Suppression, ME-Replacement, etc.
  • Contractor must be registered with City of Plantation — licensed HVAC contractor
  • Insurance COI listing 'City of Plantation' as Certificate Holder
  • Complete Mechanical Plans signed and sealed by FL licensed Engineer
  • Manual J — Load Calculation required for new installations and size changes
  • Manual N — Commercial load calculation when applicable
  • Three (3) sets of Energy Calculations signed and sealed by Engineer (Plantation requires 3 — most cities require 2)
  • Equipment specifications with AHRI certificate number
  • Product approvals / NOAs for exterior HVAC equipment (HVHZ — Miami-Dade NOAs required)
  • Digital signatures must be backed by certificate authority
  • NOCFlorida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Confirm with the building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.
  • NoteA/C changeouts (same-size replacement) route DIRECTLY to Building Division — skip Zoning
  • GotchaPlenum ceilings require specs on Structural, Electrical, Mechanical AND Plumbing plans
  • GotchaPlantation is HVHZ — exterior HVAC equipment requires valid Miami-Dade NOAs
  • GotchaWork hours 7 AM – 8 PM weekdays/Saturdays; NO Sundays or holidays
Requirements change. Confirm the current checklist with the Plantation building department before you submit. See every permit type on the Plantation permit requirements page.
Frequently asked questions
Where do I submit a permit application in Plantation?

Digital ONLY since 11/14/2022 via ACA portal — application signed and notarized by Qualifier. Portal: https://aca.plantation.org/CitizenAccess/.

What insurance certificate holder wording does Plantation require?

The certificate holder should read: City of Plantation

Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Plantation?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Plantation building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

How many plan sets does Plantation require?

3 sets listed. This can vary by permit type, so confirm with the building department.

Is Plantation in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ)?

Yes. Plantation is in the HVHZ, which affects product approval and wind load requirements.

What gets a mechanical / HVAC application rejected in Plantation?

Plenum ceilings require specs on Structural, Electrical, Mechanical AND Plumbing plans. Plantation is HVHZ — exterior HVAC equipment requires valid Miami-Dade NOAs. Work hours 7 AM – 8 PM weekdays/Saturdays; NO Sundays or holidays.
