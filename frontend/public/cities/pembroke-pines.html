Frequently asked questions
Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Pembroke Pines?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Pembroke Pines building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

What are common reasons permits get rejected in Pembroke Pines?

All applications must be NOTARIZED - missing notarization = automatic rejection. Qualifying contractor must sign application per F.S. 713.135. Cash is NOT accepted - checks/money orders payable to 'City of Pembroke Pines' only.
