2NOC must be POSTED at job site for first inspection (7 days after permit issued) — if not posted, inspection fails and $75 re-inspection fee is charged
3Inspection cancellation cutoff is 8:30 AM — cancellations after 8:30 AM may result in additional fees on the permit
4OAS (Online Application Submittal) is the REQUIRED method for all permit submissions as of January 12, 2026 — paper submissions are limited to walk-through only
5Jobs valued at $100,000+ require the application/drop-off fee to be paid BEFORE the permit is routed to plan review
6Walk-through permits (Wednesdays 8-10:30 AM) are one-time review — if rejected, the entire package is returned and must be resubmitted as regular review through OAS
7Broward County updated the standard Building Permit Form — as of 03/09/2026 the new form is REQUIRED; old forms will be rejected
8Survey Affidavit is required if survey is older than 2 years AND there have been no changes to the property — without the affidavit a new survey is needed
9NOAs must include a statement that they have been reviewed and approved by the Designer of Record (DOR)
10All applications must be notarized (owner AND contractor signatures) — un-notarized applications are rejected
11Broward Property Appraiser printout (bcpa.net) is required with every permit application — missing this delays processing
12File naming and file type in ProjectDox MUST follow instructions exactly — failure to follow directions will delay the permit
13Power will NOT be released until all required inspections are passed and all fees are paid — a 30-day temporary power is issued prior to final electrical inspection
14Digital signatures must comply with Davie's Signing and Sealing Plans Guidelines and Digital Signature Regulation & Affidavit — regular electronic signatures may be rejected
Frequently asked questions
Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Davie?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Davie building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

How many plan sets does Davie require?

2 paper sets if submitting paper plans, or submit electronically via OAS/ProjectDox.

Is Davie in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ)?

Yes. Davie is in the HVHZ, which affects product approval and wind load requirements.

What are common reasons permits get rejected in Davie?
