Frequently asked questions
Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Boynton Beach?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Boynton Beach building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.
