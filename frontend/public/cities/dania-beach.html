Common rejection reasons in Dania Beach

18 documented. Our analysis checks your package against these.

1NOTARIZATION REQUIRED: Permit applications AND Owner/Agent letters must be notarized — unsigned/unnotarized apps will be rejected at counter
2NOC TIMING CONFLICT: GFL Alliance says 'prior to submittal' but FAQ says 'prior to first inspection' — safest approach is to have recorded NOC at submittal. Record at Broward County Clerk, secure 2 certified copies
3PLANS NOT RETURNED: Dania Beach will NOT return plans for corrections. New plan sheets must be submitted to the Permitting Department — budget for extra printing costs
4SWALE AFFIDAVIT: If swale area uses anything other than asphalt, a recorded Swale Affidavit must be attached to application AND recorded at County Clerk BEFORE permit issuance — this catches driveway contractors who forget
5APPLICATION EXPIRY: 60 calendar days after last review notification with no action = void. Process fee is NON-REFUNDABLE and must be paid again
6ELEVATION CERTIFICATES: Required TWICE — after lowest floor AND at Final CO. Miss the first one and your CO gets held up
7DRC PLANS REQUIRED: Original Development Review Committee stamped/signed approved plans must be submitted (not required for interiors only)
8SPO SURCHARGE: Projects $1M+ automatically get Special Permitting Office processing with 2.5% surcharge (min $100). Director may also assign other complex projects to SPO
9FIRE INSPECTIONS SEPARATE: Fire inspections handled by BSO at 954-831-8210 — NOT the city building inspection line. Different entity, different scheduling
10ROOF-TO-WALL AFFIDAVIT: Per FBC 8th Ed Section 706.8 — prescriptive method affidavit must be completed by FL PE, RA, CGC, CBC, or CRC (NOT by roofing contractor or standard roofing inspector). Must be NOTARIZED. Photos required.
11REQUIRED OWNER'S NOTIFICATION FOR ROOFING: FBC Section 1524 (HVHZ) — contractor must provide and explain to owner. Owner must initial each item. Covers renailing, exposed ceilings, ponding water, overflow scuppers.
12INSURANCE CERT HOLDER: Must show 'City of Dania Beach' — no specific street address required but entity name must be exact
13CONTRACTOR REGISTRATION NOTARIZED: The registration form itself must be signed AND notarized — not just the permit application
14REVISIONS AFTER APPROVAL: Need Revision Submittal Form + Architect's Narrative + clouds and deltas on drawings. Signatures not required on revisions.
15EPD CONCURRENT OK: Can submit to Broward County EPD concurrently, but EPD approval MUST be received before permit issuance
16ELEVATOR CONCURRENT OK: Can submit to Broward County Elevator concurrently, but approval must be received before issuance
17ELECTRONIC PLAN REVIEW: SPO uses CAP Government IDT Plans system (cap.idtplans.com) — separate from ePermits OneStop
18SENATE BILL 612: AC contractors can now do line-side electrical work per FL Statute 489.105 (effective July 1, 2024). Electrical permit NOT required but electrical plan review and inspection BY an electrical inspector IS required. This is a Broward-wide BORA policy (Policy 19-02 revised 07/02/2024).
Frequently asked questions
Where do I submit a permit application in Dania Beach?

In-person Mon-Thu 8:30AM-3:30PM; Drop box Fri; ePermits online. Portal: https://esuite-daniabeach-fl.tylertech.com.

What insurance certificate holder wording does Dania Beach require?

The certificate holder should read: City of Dania Beach

Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Dania Beach?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Dania Beach building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

How many plan sets does Dania Beach require?

2 sets listed. This can vary by permit type, so confirm with the building department.

Is Dania Beach in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ)?

Yes. Dania Beach is in the HVHZ, which affects product approval and wind load requirements.
