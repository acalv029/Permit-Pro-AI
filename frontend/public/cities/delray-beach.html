Frequently asked questions
Where do I submit a permit application in Delray Beach?

ALL DIGITAL — 100% paperless since Oct 1, 2024. Paper plans NOT reviewed. $25 scanning fee for paper submissions.. Portal: https://cityofdelraybeachfl-energovweb.tylerhost.net/apps/selfservice#/home.

What insurance certificate holder wording does Delray Beach require?

The certificate holder should read: City of Delray Beach 100 NW 1st Avenue Delray Beach, FL 33444

Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Delray Beach?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Delray Beach building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

How many plan sets does Delray Beach require?

0 sets listed. This can vary by permit type, so confirm with the building department.
