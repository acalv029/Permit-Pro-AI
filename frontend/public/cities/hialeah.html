Frequently asked questions
Where do I submit a permit application in Hialeah?

In-person, email (permits@hialeahfl.gov), or Tyler CSS portal — all applications must be NOTARIZED. Portal: https://epay.hialeahfl.gov/.

What insurance certificate holder wording does Hialeah require?

The certificate holder should read: City of Hialeah

Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Hialeah?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Hialeah building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

How many plan sets does Hialeah require?

2 sets listed. This can vary by permit type, so confirm with the building department.

Is Hialeah in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ)?

Yes. Hialeah is in the HVHZ, which affects product approval and wind load requirements.

What are common reasons permits get rejected in Hialeah?

ALL applications MUST be NOTARIZED — strictly enforced, automatic rejection without it. Owner affidavits MUST also be NOTARIZED — both owner AND qualifier signatures. For condos: Association Authorization Letter with president's signature NOTARIZED required for ALL permits.
