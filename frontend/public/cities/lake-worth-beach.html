Common rejection reasons in Lake Worth Beach

24 documented. Our analysis checks your package against these.

1Lake Worth Beach has SIX (6) historic districts + 4 individually designated landmarks. ANY exterior work in these areas requires a Certificate of Appropriateness (COA) BEFORE a building permit can be issued. COA and building permit must be submitted CONCURRENTLY. Failure to obtain COA = stop work order.
2Pre-application meetings are REQUIRED for projects involving new construction, demolitions, or changes of use in historic districts. Pre-app strongly encouraged for major additions. Email pzhp@lakeworthbeachfl.gov to schedule.
3COA construction must commence within 12 months of issuance and 50% complete within 12 months of commencement. CO required within 24 months of commencement.
4Primary permit fees INCLUDE sub-permit fees ONLY IF all applicable subcontractor qualifier signatures are on the application AND plans include sub details. Missing a sub-qualifier signature = SEPARATE permit + SEPARATE fees for that trade.
5NOC must be RECORDED at PBC Clerk of Court AND POSTED ON JOB SITE before first inspection — not at submittal, not at issuance. Both conditions required.
6Walk-in hours are ONLY 1st & 3rd Wednesdays 8AM-12PM. Outside those windows, you cannot walk in without an appointment.
7Inspections must be requested by 4:00 PM the business day BEFORE desired date. Same-day inspections require direct call to (561) 586-1647 and carry ADDITIONAL FEES.
83rd plan review = $50 fee. 4th+ plan review = 4x Plan Filing Fee. Plan Filing Fee (50% of permit) is NON-REFUNDABLE. Getting it right the first time saves significant money.
9Plan resubmittals must be COMPLETE — partial submittals NOT accepted. Emails limited to 10MB; larger files must go through website portal.
10Building permit application requires NOTARIZATION — both owner and contractor signatures must be sworn/affirmed before a notary. Not having a notary available = no submission.
11ALL signatures on the permit application MUST BE ORIGINAL. Digital/copied signatures may be rejected.
12Contractor MUST be registered with city BEFORE pulling permits. Registration requires: PBC Occupational License, PBC Certification or State of FL Certification/Registration, Certificate of Liability Insurance, Workers Comp or Exemption, and Qualifier's Driver's License.
13Lake Worth Beach has its OWN municipal electric utility (not FPL). Service disconnects, reconnects, and coordination go through LWB Utilities Customer Service (561) 533-7300, NOT FPL.
14Driveway approach in public right-of-way requires a SEPARATE Right-of-Way Application — not just the building permit.
15LWB's permit exemption threshold is $1,000 (LOWER than the typical $2,500 FL standard). Work valued at $1,001+ requires a permit even for minor repairs.
16Lake Worth Beach is NOT in the HVHZ — it's in the Wind-Borne Debris Region. Uses FL Product Approval (FL#), NOT Miami-Dade NOA. Submitting MDC NOAs instead of FL Product Approvals = rejection.
17Pools in designated historic districts are subject to review by historic preservation staff and MAY REQUIRE a Board appearance — which will DELAY permit processing.
18Windows and doors replaced in designated historic districts are subject to review by historic preservation staff and MAY REQUIRE a Board appearance — delays processing.
19Sign permits in historic districts or on main thoroughfares require COA approval by the Historic/Zoning Board — additional delay.
20Fence permits require a Fence/Gate Removal Agreement RECORDED with PBC Clerk of Court AND a Hold Harmless/Indemnification Agreement. Both are city-specific forms. Missing either = hold.
21Finished side of fence MUST face adjacent property. If not, a notarized affidavit from affected property owner waiving the requirement must be submitted BEFORE final inspection.
22New commercial construction must have Palm Beach County IMPACT FEE STAMP prior to permit issuance. Missing this = no permit issued.
23Demolition requires Planning, Zoning and Historic Preservation APPROVAL FIRST before proceeding with any other release forms. If structure is historically significant or contributing, COA from HRPB is required FIRST.
24Requests to view city property files must be received 2 business days in advance and appointment required for non-electronic files. Email bpermits@lakeworthbeachfl.gov.
Frequently asked questions
Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Lake Worth Beach?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Lake Worth Beach building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.
