Building Permit checklist for Miramar

Frequently asked questions
Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Miramar?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Miramar building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

What gets a building application rejected in Miramar?

Building Department CLOSED ON FRIDAYS — Mon-Thur 7 AM - 6 PM only. Applications must be in BLACK INK — other ink colors will be REJECTED. Do NOT highlight any information on plans — will be REJECTED.
