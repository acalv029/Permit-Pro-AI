Frequently asked questions
Where do I submit a permit application in Kendall?

Yellow Form - signed and notarized.

Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Kendall?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Kendall building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

Is Kendall in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ)?

Yes. Kendall is in the HVHZ, which affects product approval and wind load requirements.
