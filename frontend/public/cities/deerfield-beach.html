Common rejection reasons in Deerfield Beach

21 documented. Our analysis checks your package against these.

1Required when the direct contract is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing HVAC system), per Fla. Stat. 713.135. Some older city forms list lower figures. Confirm timing with the building department.
2NOC must be RECORDED and submitted AT SUBMITTAL (prior to permit issuance) — not at first inspection like most Broward cities.
3Insurance certificate holder must read EXACTLY: 'The City of Deerfield Beach / 150 NE 2 Avenue / Deerfield Beach, FL 33441' — pulled from Contractor Registration Form PDF.
4ALL permit applications require OWNER SIGNATURE — no exceptions. Missing owner signature = automatic rejection.
5HOA Affidavit is required for ALL residential permits — even if property is NOT in an HOA. If property IS in an HOA, also need formal HOA approval letter for Zoning review.
6Product Approvals (NOAs) must be stamped 'approved' by the Designer of Record (DOR) — raw/unstamped NOAs are NOT accepted. This is stricter than most Broward cities.
7Asbestos Statement (SRRA) is MANDATORY for ALL re-roof submittals — no exceptions. Highlighted on city website.
8Condo owners CANNOT do their own work — must hire a licensed contractor. Failure is a FELONY under F.S. 489.127(1)(f).
9Service Change Procedures form must be NOTARIZED (per FBC 2023 Broward County Amendment Prov 107.3.4.0.8).
10Day of service change — must contact inspector between 7:30-8:00 AM at (305) 962-5834. DO NOT PROCEED if you do not speak directly to inspector.
11Expired permit renewal is 50% of original permit fee (from Fee Schedule Resolution 2024/202) — NOT a flat fee.
12Fence permit — if material is anything other than 6' chain link or wood, a current NOA or site-specific engineer detail is REQUIRED.
13Demolition requires 12 separate documents including Pest Control Inspection Report, EPA License for refrigerant recovery, and notarized water supply letter for dust control.
14Pool Safety Act form requires BOTH owner AND contractor signatures, BOTH must be NOTARIZED. Pool cannot be filled until BOTH final electrical AND pool barrier inspections pass.
15Solar PV permits require pictures of the existing service disconnect showing main breaker, buss, manufacturer label, and service/feeder conductors.
16Window/Door applications must be signed by qualifier AND NOTARIZED per Broward County Uniform requirements.
17Turtle Glass Requirements apply in sea turtle nesting areas — separate form/requirements from DFB.
18Generator exhaust must be minimum 10 feet from any openable openings (doors, windows, vents). Generator must be at least 5 feet from combustible walls.
19All outside agency approvals (Broward DER, SWFMD, DOH, Elevators, AHCA, etc.) must be obtained BEFORE building dept submittal — not concurrent.
20Permit card must be displayed on front of unit visible from outside during progress of work until final inspection. Original permit documents must be on-site for inspections.
21Online payments incur a transaction fee (credit/debit/check); in-person payments at City cashier do NOT. Two separate transactions will appear for online payments.
Frequently asked questions
Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Deerfield Beach?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Deerfield Beach building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.
