Frequently asked questions
Where do I submit a permit application in Coral Springs?

Electronic preferred (7 business days review) vs Hard Copy (15 business days review). Portal: https://etrakit.coralsprings.gov.

Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Coral Springs?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Coral Springs building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

How many plan sets does Coral Springs require?

2 sets listed. This can vary by permit type, so confirm with the building department.

Is Coral Springs in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ)?

Yes. Coral Springs is in the HVHZ, which affects product approval and wind load requirements.

What are common reasons permits get rejected in Coral Springs?
