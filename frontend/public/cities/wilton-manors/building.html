Building Permit checklist for Wilton Manors
  • Submit ALL permits online via Citizenserve portal (https://www6.citizenserve.com — installation ID 125)
  • Use Broward County Uniform Building Permit Application (Revised 11/14/2025) — available on city Document Center
  • Application must be signed and notarized
  • All applications must be in BLACK INK — other ink will be rejected
  • GotchaBuilding Department office historically CLOSED ON FRIDAY per GFL Alliance — open Monday through Thursday 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM (verify current hours at 954-390-2180)
  • Inspections are conducted on Fridays — call for inspector times (Angela Ruffner 954-498-8329, Mariana Fonseca 786-748-2399, Betsy Alvarez 561-873-9049)
  • Two (2) sets of plans required — signed and sealed by FL licensed architect/engineer
  • Plans to be drawn on standard blueprint/blueline paper, drawn to scale at 24x36 inches
  • One (1) copy of survey with original seal prepared within the last 2 years — showing actual lot dimensions, building locations, flood zone info including base flood elevation
  • GotchaBroward County Environmental Protection Division (EPD) submittal and approval REQUIRED PRIOR to Building Department submittal — apply online for security code and application number, walk-thru review, impact fees determined if any
  • Submit Asbestos form if interior-only project OR if renovation/addition
  • Elevator: Broward County Elevator submittal/approval required PRIOR to Building Dept submittal — submit plans only for review, no application needed, allow 1 week review time
  • NOCFlorida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Confirm with the building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.
  • Record NOC at Broward County Recording Office — secure 2 certified copies
  • Contractor Registration required for ALL contractors on project — use city Contractor Registration Form
  • Registration requires: (1) Business Tax Receipt, (2) Certificate of General Liability Insurance, (3) Certificate of Workers Compensation Insurance or State Issued Exemption, (4) State Contractor License, (5) Certificate of Competency License (if applicable), (6) Qualifier's Driver's License
  • GotchaInsurance certificate holder EXACT VERBATIM wording: 'City of Wilton Manors, 2020 Wilton Drive, Wilton Manors, FL 33305' — phone 954-390-2180, fax 954-390-2184
  • Copy of contractor's license and insurance required with EVERY permit application
  • One (1) copy of Broward County Property Appraiser information sheet (bcpa.net — Property Search — print screen)
  • Copy of signed contract
  • Owner/Agent Letter required — must have Owner AND Agent notarized signatures
  • Condo/Homeowner Association Approval letter (if applicable)
  • Special Inspection Forms — must be signed and sealed by engineer AND signed by Owner (not required for interiors only)
  • One (1) copy of Impervious & Pervious Space Calculation form (required for additions, driveways, pools, decks, patios, sheds, walkways)
  • NoteRS-5 Single Family Zoning: maximum 50% of entire property can be impervious
  • Structural calculations — signed and sealed by engineer (not required for interiors only)
  • Energy calculations — signed and sealed by engineer (not required for interiors only)
  • Design wind load calculations — signed and sealed
  • Soil tests (for new construction)
  • Elevation certificate (for additions in flood zones)
  • HVHZMiami-Dade County NOAs (Notices of Acceptance) required — stamped by architect for windows, doors, louvers, shutters, garage doors, roofing materials
  • Product approvals required for garage doors, roofing material, windows, doors, storm shutters
  • Storm shutters: Dade County or Florida Product Approval AND engineering required
  • Roof calculations (HVC) — must be complete package with roof NOAs and roof plan (not required for interiors only)
  • Roof truss shop drawings — signed and sealed by engineer, include all calcs (not required for interiors only)
  • Original Development Review Committee (DRC) stamped/approved plans required — must submit stamped/original signed approved plans (not required for interiors only)
  • Fire sprinkler/alarm drawings — must have hydraulic calcs, piping diagram, and cut sheets of all devices and panels
  • Owner/Builder Affidavit available — MUST be signed IN PERSON at Community Development Services Department, cannot be submitted online
  • GotchaOwner/Builder can build/improve one-family or two-family residence for OWN use only — cannot be sold or leased within 1 year after construction complete (presumption of violation)
  • Owner/Builder cannot build commercial buildings if cost exceeds $75,000
  • Owner/Builder must provide direct onsite supervision — cannot delegate to unlicensed contractor
  • GotchaRevisions require: (1) new permit application, (2) plans s/s by ORIGINAL design professional, (3) Architect's Narrative, (4) clouds and deltas on plans, (5) updated permit application
  • ALL inspection requests must be submitted online prior to 4:30 PM for next business day
  • Zoning inspections conducted on Tuesdays and Thursdays ONLY (as of October 1, 2025)
  • Building services by Calvin, Giordano & Associates (SAFEBUILT Company) — contact plan reviewer or inspector directly for comments
  • FBC 8th Edition (2023) — effective January 1, 2024 — all permits submitted on/after this date must be designed under 8th Edition
  • NoteCity is designated GFL Alliance Platinum City for streamlined permitting
  • Building Safety Inspection Program (BSIP) applies per FBC Chapter 1 Section 110.15 — milestone/recertification inspections for buildings 30+ years old
  • BSIP requires both Structural Inspection Form and Electrical Inspection Form (per Broward BORA Policy 05-05)
  • Notice of Termite Protective Treatment required — final treatment must be complete before final inspection
  • FeeConstruction cost up to $1,000 = $150.00 per structure per trade
  • FeeConstruction cost $1,001–$1,250,000 = $150 + 2% of cost over $1,000
  • FeeConstruction cost $1,250,001–$3,000,000 = $21,000 + 1.6% of cost over $1,250,000
  • FeeConstruction cost over $3,000,000 = $48,000 + 1.5% of cost over $3,000,000
  • FeeChange of contractor = $125
  • FeePermit extension (prior to expiration) = $125
  • FeeExpired permit renewal (within 6 months, same permit/plans/property, no refund made) = 50% of original construction fee
  • FeeRe-inspection — 1st occurrence: $100, 2nd: $125, 3rd and all subsequent: $150 per trade
  • FeeCertificate of Completion/Occupancy = $150
  • FeeTemporary Certificate of Completion/Occupancy = $450
  • FeeReplacement permit inspection card = $45
  • FeeTechnology Access Fee = $20 per permit
  • FeeBORA fee = greater of $2.00 or $0.52 per $1,000 of total construction cost
  • FeeEducational fee = 0.03% of cost of construction
  • FeeBCAIF surcharge = 1.5% of all permit fees (min $2.00) per F.S. 468.631
  • FeeFL Building Permit Surcharge = 1% of all permit fees (min $2.00) per F.S. 553.721
  • FeeBSIP 40-year safety inspection = $350 per building
  • GotchaWork without permit — 1st offense: DOUBLE the construction fee; 2nd offense: DOUBLE + $275; each offense thereafter: DOUBLE + $687
  • GotchaPlan rejection penalty — if plans rejected 3+ times for SAME code violation, fee is 4x the plan review portion of permit fee (per F.S. 553.80(2)(b))
  • NoteRefunds — no refund for first $1,000 of construction value; 25% refund available only if permit not expired, no work started, no inspections recorded, and written request on file
  • NoteGreen Building Program Incentive — 50% reduction in base building permit fee for residential retrofits meeting ULDR Article 170 requirements (at final inspection approval)
  • NoteBuilding Official may use RS Means Construction Valuation to verify stated construction cost — the GREATER of applicant's value, contract, or RS Means is used
  • Digital Signature Affidavit required when using digital signatures on sealed documents

Where do I submit a permit application in Wilton Manors?

What insurance certificate holder wording does Wilton Manors require?

Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Wilton Manors?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Wilton Manors building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

How many plan sets does Wilton Manors require?

2 sets listed. This can vary by permit type, so confirm with the building department.

Is Wilton Manors in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ)?

Yes. Wilton Manors is in the HVHZ, which affects product approval and wind load requirements.
