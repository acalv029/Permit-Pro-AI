Common rejection reasons in Miami Gardens

35 documented. Our analysis checks your package against these.

1CLOSED FRIDAYS — Building Services is open Mon–Thu 7:00 AM – 6:00 PM ONLY. Contractors who show up on Friday lose a full day.
2Must sign in before 5:00 PM for permit processing. After 5 PM you WILL NOT be processed even if you're in the building.
330% UP-FRONT NON-REFUNDABLE processing fee on ALL permits — this is collected at application, not at issuance.
4EXPIRED PERMIT BLOCK — If a contractor has ANY expired permit at ANY Miami Gardens property, they may be BLOCKED from pulling permits for ANY new project. Check ALL your MG permits before applying.
5DERM APPROVAL BEFORE CITY PERMIT — New buildings, nonresidential additions, commercial interiors, commercial re-roofs, commercial pools, land clearing, demolition ALL require DERM first. Submitting without DERM = automatic rejection.
6Permit application form says 'ALL FIELDS MUST BE FILLED IN OR APPLICATION WILL BE DENIED PROCESS' — no blanks allowed, period.
7Insurance cert must be made out to 'City of Miami Gardens' — the Registration PDF says 'CERTIFICATE OF INSURANCE MUST BE MADE OUT TO THE CITY OF MIAMI GARDENS' — exact entity name matters.
8Contractor Registration requires qualifier's driver's license copy — not just license and insurance.
9County-licensed contractors need 6 documents (Certificate of Competency, FL State Registration, GL Insurance, WC/Exemption, MDC Occupational License, Driver's License) vs state-licensed contractors who need 5.
10Owner agents must have an affidavit on file OR submit one with the permit application — no exceptions.
11HOA/Condo Affidavit is REQUIRED on every single application — even if the property is NOT in an association (you check the 'am not' box).
123 failed inspections = potential Stop Work Order + requirement to hire licensed contractor to complete work.
13Work without permit = DOUBLE FEE + $126 penalty first offense; $252.50 second; $631.50 each after. AND the double fee requirement applies to ALL trades.
14Solar permits require BOTH Building AND Electrical applications — plus a SEPARATE electrical application if service is being upgraded.
15Solar permits require Special Inspector Form for connections and waterproofing — this is unique to Miami Gardens.
16Solar CVC contractor can install PV equipment EXCEPT the interconnection to FPL power source — that requires EC/ER electrical contractor with SEPARATE permit.
17AC replacement forms are PER SYSTEM — each unit gets its own form. AHRI certificate required for each.
18Fence permits — PVC and Metal fences require signed/sealed plans by FL architect/engineer. Wood and chain link can use prescriptive FBC method but STILL need fence details or requirements sheet.
19All fence types require Easement Encroachment Affidavit — even if there's no apparent easement. Property owner must execute this.
20Fence gate location — if the marked area on the survey for a gate does NOT have an existing driveway, a gate will NOT be permitted.
21Pool permits need ALL four trade applications: Building + Mechanical (if applicable) + Electrical + Plumbing — submitted as a package.
22Pool Safety Act form must be notarized by BOTH owner/agent AND prime contractor — two separate notarizations on one form.
23OSHA-approved ladder REQUIRED at all job sites for ALL inspections — $120.00 fine for non-compliant ladder at time of inspection.
24Roofing — NOA/Product Approval must be provided at time of permit application AND posted at jobsite for inspection. Missing either = rejection.
25Roofing — Tile roof re-roofs require pull test PRIOR to final inspection.
26Roofing — Roof-to-wall connection retrofit required for structures with insured value over $300,000 (F.S. 553.844). Must provide exemption affidavit if under $300K.
27Commercial re-roofing with system change requires signed/sealed structural calculations for supporting structure.
28Private Provider — if PP does plan review, they MUST also do inspections (Building Official's discretion per F.S. 553.791(2)(a)). Pre-construction meeting with Building Official REQUIRED before permit issuance.
29Demolition requires disconnect letters from ALL utilities: FPL, WASD water meter, cable, Florida City Gas/TECO if applicable — plus DOH permit for septic tank abandonment.
30Driveway/slab permits require Impervious Percentage Form — unique city form that must be calculated and submitted.
31New construction and additions BOTH require DERM approval AND Special Inspector Form — submitting without these = rejection.
32Shed maximum size is 120 SF with specific setbacks: 7.5' side, 5' rear, 10' from house. Exceeding = denied.
33The permit application form MUST be notarized — owner, lessee, AND qualifier sections all require notarization. Un-notarized = denied.
34Permit renewal requires expired permit not to be older than 6 months AND that no refund was previously issued. After 6 months = full new permit.
35$500 Early Start fee per master permit — work cannot proceed past first required inspection even with Building Official approval.
Frequently asked questions
Where do I submit a permit application in Miami Gardens?

Online via CSS portal — all attachments uploaded digitally; payments online only. Portal: https://miamigardensfl-energovpub.tylerhost.net/apps/selfservice#/home.

Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Miami Gardens?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Miami Gardens building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

How many plan sets does Miami Gardens require?

2 sets listed. This can vary by permit type, so confirm with the building department.

Is Miami Gardens in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ)?

Yes. Miami Gardens is in the HVHZ, which affects product approval and wind load requirements.
