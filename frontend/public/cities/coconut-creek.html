Frequently asked questions
Where do I submit a permit application in Coconut Creek?

Electronic via ePermits; owner-builders must apply IN-PERSON. Portal: https://www.coconutcreek.gov/business/permits-and-payments/epermits.

Do I need a Notice of Commencement in Coconut Creek?

Florida law requires a recorded Notice of Commencement when the contract price is greater than $5,000 ($15,000 for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system). Timing can vary, so confirm with the Coconut Creek building department whether it is due at application or before the first inspection.

How many plan sets does Coconut Creek require?

2 sets listed. This can vary by permit type, so confirm with the building department.

Is Coconut Creek in the High Velocity Hurricane Zone (HVHZ)?

Yes. Coconut Creek is in the HVHZ, which affects product approval and wind load requirements.

What are common reasons permits get rejected in Coconut Creek?

Building Department is CLOSED ON FRIDAYS — open Mon–Thu 7AM–6PM only. Applications must be in BLACK INK — blue ink or pencil will be rejected. PDF Portfolio uploads NOT compatible with ePermits — must be regular unlocked PDF files.
