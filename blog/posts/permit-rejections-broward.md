A permit package can be rejected for paperwork reasons that have nothing to do with the quality of the work: the wrong ink, a missing seal, an insurance certificate with the wrong wording. Each Broward city publishes its own rules, so the same package can pass in one city and fail in the next. These ten come from the city checklists we track, including Fort Lauderdale, Pompano Beach, Wilton Manors and Plantation, and from official Broward County pages.

## 1. Plans that are not signed and sealed the way the city wants

Fort Lauderdale requires signed and sealed structural calculations for all new residential structures. For revisions, it asks for a new permit application, an architect's narrative, clouds and deltas on the plans, an "extra" stamped set, the owner's signature on the application, and a revision date and number on every revised sheet, with voided sheets removed. Read the revision rules before you resubmit, not after.

## 2. The wrong ink or an outdated form

Fort Lauderdale says forms must be completed in black ink, and only signatures may be in standard blue ink. Wilton Manors requires black ink on all applications and rejects other colors. Pompano Beach's roofing checklist says applications must be in black ink or they will be rejected. Cities also name the form revision they want: Wilton Manors points to the Broward uniform application revised 11/14/2025, and Pompano Beach's roofing checklist calls for a BORA revision dated January 8, 2026.

## 3. Product approvals (NOAs) that are not submitted correctly

Fort Lauderdale requires a Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance for all roofing products, and says to circle the relevant information, never highlight it, because highlighted NOAs are rejected. It also asks for all sheets of each NOA, and says NOAs must be approved by the designer of record when they are part of a full set of plans. See our [HVHZ explainer](/blog/hvhz-explained-broward-miami-dade.html) for why these approvals matter in Broward.

## 4. A missing or incorrect Notice of Commencement

Broward County says a Notice of Commencement is required when the total contract price is greater than $5,000, and not required for repair or replacement of an existing heating or air conditioning system under $15,000. A recorded copy can be submitted with the application or made available on the job site at the first inspection. Our [Notice of Commencement guide](/blog/notice-of-commencement-florida-broward-miami-dade.html) covers the details.

## 5. Plans that reference the wrong code edition

Wilton Manors' checklist says the Florida Building Code, 8th Edition (2023) took effect January 1, 2024, and that permits submitted on or after that date must be designed under it. If you reuse plan templates, make sure your engineer updated the code reference.

## 6. Missing calculations and certificates

Fort Lauderdale requires energy calculations (two copies) for a change of occupancy, a change in space conditioning, and renovations of 30% or more of the assessed value of the structure. For an A/C changeout, Wilton Manors requires an AHRI certificate for the new unit.

## 7. Insurance certificate holder wording

Fort Lauderdale's holder must read exactly: City of Fort Lauderdale, 700 NW 19th Avenue, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33311, and it says a wrong name, wrong address or abbreviations mean rejection. Wilton Manors wants: City of Wilton Manors, 2020 Wilton Drive, Wilton Manors, FL 33305. Pompano Beach's checklist lists: City of Pompano Beach, 100 W Atlantic Blvd, Pompano Beach, FL 33060. Each city's page on Flo Permit shows the wording we have on file, and it is worth confirming with the city before you send it to your insurance agent.

## 8. A missing or outdated survey

Fort Lauderdale requires a current signed and sealed survey and a site plan locating all proposed work for exterior work. Wilton Manors asks for a survey within the last two years with the original seal. The age limit varies by city, so check yours.

## 9. Details that do not match across documents

Plantation says its staff verify the owner, contractor and property address on the Notice of Commencement against the permit application. Fort Lauderdale will not issue permits to contractors with expired credentials, and an expired license does not appear in a LauderBuild search. Before you submit, check that the license, insurance, NOC and application all show the same names and are current.

## 10. Digital submission mistakes

Fort Lauderdale is 100% digital through LauderBuild, and paper applications are not accepted. Each insurance document must be uploaded as its own PDF in the correct category, not combined into one file. Wilton Manors takes every permit through its Citizenserve portal. Check the upload rules for your city's portal before you assemble the package.

## What to do about it

Treat your city's checklist as the source of truth, and check your package against it before you submit. Our [city requirement pages](/cities/) list each city's permit types, insurance wording and rejection reasons.
